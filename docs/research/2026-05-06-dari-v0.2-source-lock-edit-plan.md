## Editorial North Star

The DARI note should make the database project more credible, not make Bitcoin look artificially good. The claim is not "Bitcoin can absorb global curtailment." The stronger and more durable claim is:

> Renewable curtailment is large, heterogeneous, and poorly consolidated across jurisdictions. Bitcoin mining is one possible flexible-load use case, but the first public good is a verifiable open curtailment database with explicit source quality, definitions, and uncertainty.

## Required Reframe

### From

"Under a central illustrative trajectory, curtailed renewable electricity becomes comparable to Bitcoin network demand within the 2025-2030 horizon."

### To

"The coverage-ratio framework shows how to test whether curtailed renewable electricity is large enough to matter for flexible computational load. The current public release should treat global crossing-year examples as didactic scenarios until the ELJ database has a source-locked global numerator."

## Add A "What We Are Not Claiming" Box

Place after the Summary.

Use content like:

- We are not claiming that Bitcoin mining can absorb global renewable curtailment.
- We are not claiming curtailed electricity is automatically low-carbon once associated with mining.
- We are not claiming annual curtailed-energy totals are deployable at real sites.
- We are not claiming current global curtailment is 58.3 TWh or will be 100 TWh by 2030.
- We are not treating lower-quality regional estimates as source-verified totals.
- We are proposing a measurement framework and an open database with explicit tiers, uncertainty bands, and source provenance.

## Journal Split

Do not use DARI v0.2 as the journal manuscript. The journal path needs one of these supply-side replacements:

1. Bottom-up ELJ-derived range, tier-separated and reproducible.
2. IEA or other institutional scenario inputs, clearly parametric and not claimed as ELJ observation.
3. Per-jurisdiction coverage ratios, avoiding a fragile global numerator.

The preferred path is 1. If 1 is not ready, the journal should pivot to 3 rather than publishing a weak global total.
