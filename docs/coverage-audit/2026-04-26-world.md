## Weak-coverage flag

Four of the ten dispatch zones produced **zero `introduce-as-T1` candidates**: Africa, North-America, Oceania-Pacific, and Europe-ENTSO-E. These are not gaps in the audit — they reflect genuine structural realities:

- **Europe-ENTSO-E**: The existing `entsoe.json.ts` loader already covers every machine-readable A75/A77 transparency feed; remaining gaps are anchor coverage, not loader coverage.
- **North-America**: ISOs/RTOs are structurally well-covered; non-ISO BAs lack curtailment publication.
- **Africa**: PDF-only or no-public-data is the modal classification; only Eskom publishes operator-direct hourly data.
- **Oceania-Pacific**: AEMO + EMI cover the bulk; Pacific island utilities are too small + transparency-poor for a Pattern-A loader.

The audit captures these zones primarily as documented gaps for future rounds rather than round-2 promotion targets.

## Limitations of this audit

- **Probe results are dated.** A row marked `403 / Cloudflare-challenged` today may become `200` after Cloudflare config changes. CAMMESA Argentina, NLDC Vietnam, NSMO Vietnam, NGCP Philippines, EPGE Myanmar, EDTL Timor-Leste, and ~11 Indian RLDC/SLDC sites returned timeouts or refused connections — these are flagged `unreachable` rather than `no-public-data`.
- **Sub-national disagreements stay sub-national.** Where a federal operator (POSOCO India) coexists with state operators (KSLDC, GSLDC), both appear as separate rows; the audit does not resolve which is the canonical curtailment-publishing entity. Round-2 implementation work makes that call per-region.
- **China provincial portals (~40 unprobed)** are uniformly classified using prior catalogued knowledge of NEA bulletins + login-walled SGCC/CSG SPA — these classifications carry higher uncertainty than the probed rows.
- **Microstates and Antarctica are out of scope** per spec §Scope.
- **The 250-row sanity floor passed at 345**, but four dispatch zones (Africa, North-America, Oceania-Pacific, Europe-ENTSO-E) produced no `introduce-as-T1` candidates. Surfaced in the weak-coverage flag above; not an audit defect.
