Globe approach mockups

Three rendering approaches for the Every Last Joule dashboard globe, with representative regional hotspots. Slow auto-rotation on all three.

Option A

D3-geo orthographic

Country polygons filled and outlined. D3's native orthographic projection with Natural Earth 110m country data. Day/night terminator as a radial gradient. Lightweight (D3 is already loaded). Data-journalism idiom used by NYT, Guardian, FT.

Renewable curtailment Flared gas
Option B

Three.js / Globe.gl 3D

True 3D globe via Globe.gl (wraps Three.js). Country polygons extruded in 3D. Supports zoom, tilt, free rotation. Cinematic. Heavier bundle (~600KB for Three.js).

Renewable curtailment Flared gas
Option C

Dotted + country borders

Design's dotted aesthetic preserved. Sphere of dots on land only (using real land mask, not ellipses). Thin country border lines overlaid. Hotspots as glowing blobs. Keeps the Stacked visual identity; adds recognisability.

Renewable curtailment Flared gas
